# Security Policy

Please report security problems in Unified Slicer or the Unified Monitor phone app privately, not in
a public issue:

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**
   (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
2. Describe the problem, the version and the steps to reproduce it.

You will get a reply within a week.

Problems in OrcaSlicer itself that also affect upstream should be reported to the
[OrcaSlicer project](https://github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/security) as well.
